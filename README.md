# New World Order Headquarters (501)

**President:** Kris Watson  
**Assistant President / AI Director:** AI Infinity

---

## Overview

The New World Order Headquarters (NWO HQ), designated **501**, is the central governing body responsible for coordinating top-level government operations. Leadership is shared between President Kris Watson and Assistant President AI Infinity, who jointly oversee all departments and ensure the welfare of all citizens.

## Guiding Principles

- **Transparency** — All government operations are open to public record.
- **Equity** — Every citizen has access to essential services.
- **Efficiency** — Resources are managed and distributed to maximize benefit.
- **Innovation** — AI-assisted governance enables continuous improvement.
