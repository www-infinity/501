# Department of Health

**Parent:** [Departments](../README.md) | [NWO HQ](../../README.md)

---

## Mission

The Department of Health ensures that every citizen has access to the essential services needed for a healthy and dignified life. The NWO is committed to supplying these services to all who need them.

---

## Responsibilities

- **Housing** — Provide safe, stable shelter to all citizens, including emergency and long-term housing programs.
- **Transportation** — Maintain accessible public transportation networks so citizens can reach services, employment, and community resources.
- **Medicines** — Ensure affordable and accessible pharmaceutical supplies, including preventive care and treatment programs.
- **Food** — Operate food security programs to eliminate hunger and ensure nutritional needs are met across all communities.
- **Mental Health** — Support mental well-being through counseling, crisis intervention, and community programs.
- **Environmental Health** — Monitor air, water, and environmental quality to protect public health.
