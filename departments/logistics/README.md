# Department of Logistics

**Parent:** [Departments](../README.md) | [NWO HQ](../../README.md)

---

## Mission

The Department of Logistics ensures that resources, supplies, and services reach every citizen efficiently and reliably. It coordinates the supply chain, distribution networks, and operational infrastructure that support all other government departments.

---

## Responsibilities

- **Supply Chain Management** — Plan and oversee the procurement and flow of goods across all departments.
- **Distribution Networks** — Maintain the infrastructure for delivering food, medicines, and other essentials to communities.
- **Inventory Control** — Track national stockpiles and ensure adequate reserves for all critical supplies.
- **Emergency Response Logistics** — Coordinate rapid deployment of resources during crises and natural disasters.
- **Inter-Departmental Coordination** — Facilitate communication and resource sharing between Treasury, Health, and other departments.
- **Transportation Infrastructure** — Maintain roads, rail, ports, and air logistics networks used for government operations.

---

## Key Programs

| Program | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| National Distribution Grid | Centralized network for routing supplies to all regions |
| Emergency Stockpile Program | Maintains reserves of food, medicine, and essentials for crisis response |
| Last-Mile Delivery Initiative | Ensures remote and underserved areas receive full access to government supplies |
| Logistics Technology Platform | AI-powered tools for real-time tracking and optimization of supply chains |
