# Department of Treasury

**Parent:** [Departments](../README.md) | [NWO HQ](../../README.md)

---

## Mission

The Department of Treasury manages the financial resources of the New World Order Headquarters. It is responsible for maintaining economic stability, funding all government departments, and ensuring equitable distribution of wealth across the population.

---

## Responsibilities

- **National Budget** — Allocate funding to all departments and programs.
- **Revenue Collection** — Oversee taxation, tariffs, and other income streams.
- **Economic Policy** — Develop policies to sustain long-term economic growth.
- **Monetary Oversight** — Manage the national currency and financial reserves.
- **Debt Management** — Monitor and manage national obligations and liabilities.
- **Audit & Accountability** — Ensure all departmental spending is properly recorded and justified.

---

## Key Programs

| Program | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Universal Basic Funding | Ensures every citizen has access to minimum financial resources |
| Infrastructure Investment | Funds public works, transportation, and housing construction |
| Health & Welfare Grants | Direct funding to the Department of Health programs |
| Emergency Reserve Fund | Maintained for disaster response and unforeseen crises |
